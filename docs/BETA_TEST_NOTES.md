# First beta notes

Copy these into App Store Connect after the build has processed. Add the
account holder's private contact details there, not in this public document.

## Beta description

ScorePrep helps you read and practice from your own sheet music on iPhone and
iPad. Import a PDF or photo, prepare complete reading areas, confirm their
timing, and practice with current/upcoming notation, a metronome, count-in,
speed controls and A–B loops. Original notation and markings stay intact.
The included exercise works offline. Automatic line changes follow the timing
you confirm. Live listening and voice control are not included in this beta.

## What to test

Start with Library → Open practice exercise → Practice → Play.

- Check that notation and Play/Pause/Next remain usable in portrait and landscape.
- Hear the count-in and clicks; compare audible and silent practice.
- Pause, resume, move to the next area, change speed and try an A–B loop.
- Open Practice options with Larger Text enabled in system accessibility settings.
- Import your own PDF, JPEG or PNG from Files. Prepare complete systems, confirm
  their timing, then close and reopen the app to check local persistence.

Send feedback through TestFlight with the device, orientation, steps and a
screenshot using the included exercise where possible. Avoid attaching private
or copyrighted scores without permission.

## Review notes

No account login is required for local reading and the included practice
exercise. New score recognition requires a separately configured authenticated
HTTPS worker; no public recognition service is included. The beta can be
evaluated completely using the included exercise without that optional service.
Parent/account-holder contact information, export compliance and final privacy
responses are completed in App Store Connect before the corresponding review.
